Method

Reference geometry. Streets are OpenStreetMap drivable ways (motorway through residential, plus links) fetched once from Overpass and cached beside the run. Offsets are perpendicular distances to the nearest segment within 30 m. Signed offsets are measured across the two families of the street grid, whose bearing is estimated from the segments themselves (); segments diagonal to the grid contribute only to the unsigned cross-track distance.

Counterfactuals. Each point is translated by its own pano's (field − submitted) vector. Heading is unchanged, so this isolates the position field. When a server label feed is supplied the points are the server's placements; otherwise they are the run's operational detections raycast to flat ground with the repository's own geometry.

Generated by position_check.py in the sidewalk-auto-labeler repository — automatically at the end of every main.py run, or by hand as scripts/position_check.py --report.
